brown butter chocolate chip cookies

crispy edges, chewy center, nutty from the brown butter

ingredients

  • a whole stick of butter (brown it)
  • a lot of brown sugar
  • a little less white sugar
  • 2 eggs + 1 extra yolk for good luck
  • a heroic pour of vanilla (scary!)
  • flour until the dough looks like dough
  • a pinch of baking soda
  • salt (do not skip)
  • chocolate chips — as many as feels right, then add more

steps

  • brown the butter in a saucepan over medium heat until golden and nutty smelling. let cool for 10 min
  • whisk browned butter with both sugars until combined. add eggs, yolk, and vanilla and whisk until smooth
  • fold in flour, baking soda, and salt until just combined. stir in chocolate chips
  • chill dough in fridge for at least 1 hour (overnight is better)
  • preheat oven to 375°F. scoop dough into balls and bake 10–12 min until edges are set but centers look underdone
  • let cool on pan for 5 min before moving — they firm up as they cool

notes

  • don't skip chilling — it makes a huge difference in texture
  • pull them out when they look slightly underbaked. trust
  • flaky salt on top before baking is very worth it
